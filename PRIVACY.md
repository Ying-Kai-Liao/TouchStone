# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Overview

TouchStone ("the App") is a productivity and focus management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection and Storage

### Local Data
All your personal data is stored **locally on your device**:
- Projects and phases
- Focus sessions and touch logs
- Scheduled events (stones)
- User preferences and settings

**We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any external servers.**

### API Keys
If you choose to use AI-powered features, your OpenAI API key is:
- Stored securely in your device's Keychain
- Never transmitted to our servers
- Used only for direct communication between your device and OpenAI

## Third-Party Services

### OpenAI (Optional)
If you enable AI features:
- Your project data may be sent to OpenAI for processing
- This is a direct connection between your device and OpenAI
- Please review [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy) for their data practices

### Apple Services
The App may use standard Apple frameworks:
- Calendar integration (with your permission)
- Local notifications
- iCloud sync (if enabled by you)

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:
- Sell your personal data
- Share your data with third parties for marketing
- Track your usage for advertising purposes
- Collect analytics without your knowledge

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- **Access**: All data is visible within the App
- **Delete**: Remove any data through the App's interface
- **Export**: Your data remains on your device
- **Portability**: Data is stored in standard formats

## Children's Privacy

TouchStone does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App is designed for general productivity use.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Ying-Kai-Liao/TouchStone/issues).

---

**Summary**: TouchStone keeps your data on your device. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information.
