# UX Guardrails (Non-negotiable)

## Daily entry must be zero-commitment
- Opening Today must NOT require allocating time/coins/units.
- No “Start Day” rituals that gate everything.

## Touch is acknowledgement, not commitment
- Touch is low-risk and reversible.
- Mis-taps must not matter; always provide Undo.
- Do not treat touch as a promise, plan, or completion.

## No planner behaviors in daily UI
Do NOT add:
- time-slot scheduling for projects
- overdue / missed / failure states
- progress bars or completion %
- streaks / gamification
- “you should do X next” ordering
- red warnings that imply wrongdoing

## Focus Mode is a container, not a task
- Optional after a touch
- Can exit anytime with no penalty
- No strict countdown or minute-level tracking
- “Leave early” is equal to “Finish”

## Tone and copy
- Permission-based, non-judgmental
- No coaching tone, no pressure language
- Avoid “optimize”, “maximize”, “discipline”, “accountability”
